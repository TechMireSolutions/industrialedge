# Site Identity & Logo Rules

- **Brand Name**: Always use **IndustrialEdge** (single word, PascalCase with capital `I` and capital `E`).
- **Official Logo**: Always use the official geometric delta mark logo located at `/images/logo-icon.png` (or `/logo.png`).
- **Never** replace this logo with external placeholders or other theme logos (such as Onsus).
